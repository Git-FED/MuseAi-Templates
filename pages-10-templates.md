# 10 PAGE Templates

Pages are spaces that SHOW information. They are static — they display what you put in them and do not remember anything between visits. Like a poster, a menu board, or a sign on a screen. Each template below has a ready-to-paste "Ask your Muse" prompt.

---

## 1. Food Truck Live Menu Board

**TYPE:** PAGE
**For:** A food truck owner who wants a bright menu customers can read from the sidewalk.

**What it does:** A colourful, big-text menu page showing today's items, prices, and what is sold out, designed to be displayed on a tablet at the window.

**Key features:**
- Large readable item names and prices, grouped by mains, sides, and drinks
- A "sold out" badge style for items that are gone
- A daily special highlighted at the top with a different colour
- Allergy and dietary icons next to each item
- Opening hours and next location at the bottom

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create a PAGE (a space that shows information, static, no live state): a
Food Truck Live Menu Board. Big readable text, items grouped by mains, sides,
and drinks, sold-out badges, a highlighted daily special, allergy icons, and
opening hours plus next location at the bottom. Colourful and readable from
a distance on a tablet screen.
```

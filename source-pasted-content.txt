# 10 DOCUMENT Templates

Documents are files: you read them, edit them, print them, send them. They do not run anything. Each template below has a ready-to-paste "Ask your Muse" prompt.

---

## 1. Landlord Move-In Condition Report

**TYPE:** DOCUMENT
**For:** A landlord with 1–20 rental units.

**What it does:** A room-by-room inspection form the landlord and tenant both sign on move-in day, so nobody argues later about who scratched the floor.

**Key features:**
- Room-by-room checklist (walls, floors, windows, appliances, fixtures) with a condition grade per item
- Photo log slots with a place to note the file name of each photo
- Meter readings section for water, electric, and gas on move-in day
- Signature and date lines for landlord and tenant, plus a notes box for anything unusual
- A matching move-out column so the same form is reused at the end of the tenancy

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create a DOCUMENT: a Landlord Move-In Condition Report as a printable document.
Room-by-room checklist with condition grades, photo log slots, utility meter
readings, signature lines for landlord and tenant, and a matching move-out
column. Make it clean and ready to print on letter paper.
```

- A "what to change next week" notes box at the bottom

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create a DOCUMENT: a Food Truck Weekly Cost Sheet as a document with tables.
Daily sales with cash/card split, food and supply costs, fixed costs like fuel
and commissary fees, and a profit summary showing profit per hour worked.
One page per week, ready to print and fill in by hand or on screen.

**Ask your Muse:**
```

**Key features:**

- Monthly income total across all units, shown at a glance
- Late-fee calculator that adds the fee after the grace period
- A history view so you can see every payment and repair for any unit

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create an APP (a space that does things, interactive, with its own live
database): a Landlord Rent & Repair Tracker. Tenant list per unit with rent
and paid/overdue status, repair tickets tenants can open and I can close,
monthly income totals, a late-fee calculator, and a full history per unit.
Keep it simple enough to update from my phone.
```

# Muse AI Templates

A collection of ready-to-use templates for Muse AI. Plain Markdown files — no account, no app, no Muse needed to read them. Copy any "Ask your Muse" prompt and send it to your Muse to build it.

## How Muse organizes things (in plain words)

**Artifacts hold docs and spaces. Spaces hold pages and apps.**

- A **DOCUMENT** is a file. A report, a plan, a letter, a contract, a checklist. You open it, read it, edit it, print it, send it. It does not run anything.
- A **PAGE** is a space that shows information. It is static — it displays what you put in it and does not remember anything between visits. Think of it like a poster, a menu board, or a printed sign that happens to be on a screen.
- An **APP** is a space that does things. It is interactive and live — it keeps its own data, remembers what you typed, and updates when you use it. Think of it like a machine with buttons and a memory.

The difference in one line: a document you read, a page you look at, an app you use.

## How to use a template

1. Pick a template below.
2. Copy its **Ask your Muse** prompt.
3. Paste it to your Muse.
4. Tell Muse your real details (names, numbers, dates) so it builds the thing for you, not for a stranger.

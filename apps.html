APP COLLECTION

Apps that remember

A dedicated HTML page for this collection. Every prompt is local, readable, and ready to copy.

APP01

Landlord Rent & Repair Tracker

Tracks who paid, who is late, and which repairs are open, per unit, per month — with a running balance for each tenant.

Show prompt
Create an APP (a space that does things, interactive, with its own live database): a Landlord Rent & Repair Tracker. Tenant list per unit with rent and paid/overdue status, repair tickets tenants can open and I can close, monthly income totals, a late-fee calculator, and a full history per unit. Keep it simple enough to update from my phone.
APP02

Food Truck Daily Sales Log

A fast daily log: enter what sold, what ran out, and what the till says, and it builds a running picture of the best sellers and the best locations.

Show prompt
Create an APP (a space that does things, interactive, with its own live database): a Food Truck Daily Sales Log. Quick-entry form for items sold, a location picker, sold-out flags, running best-seller and best-location totals, and an end-of-day cash vs card split. Fast enough to finish in two minutes after a shift.
APP03

Church Donation & Pledge Tracker

Records donations and pledges, shows progress toward the annual budget, and gives each giver a simple year-to-date total when asked.

Show prompt
Create an APP (a space that does things, interactive, with its own live database): a Church Donation & Pledge Tracker. Donation entry by giver, amount, date, and fund, pledge tracking with paid vs remaining, a budget progress bar, weekly offering totals, and a giver summary for year-end statements. Private and simple for a volunteer treasurer.
APP04

Freelancer Client & Invoice Board

A board of clients, projects, and invoices — showing what is in progress, what is billed, what is paid, and what is overdue.

Show prompt
Create an APP (a space that does things, interactive, with its own live database): a Freelancer Client & Invoice Board. Client cards with projects, project status from quoted to paid, an invoice list with overdue flags, monthly income from paid invoices, and a notes field per client. One screen that tells me who owes me what.
APP05

Teacher Gradebook & Attendance App

Records attendance and grades per student per class, calculates averages, and flags students who are slipping.

Show prompt
Create an APP (a space that does things, interactive, with its own live database): a Teacher Gradebook & Attendance App. Class rosters with one-tap attendance, weighted grade entry, automatic averages, flags for students missing classes or slipping in grades, and a parent-view summary. Built for a teacher with several classes.
APP06

Mechanic Bay Booking App

A booking board: customers pick a day and a bay, jobs are scheduled by length, and the day's work is visible at a glance.

Show prompt
Create an APP (a space that does things, interactive, with its own live database): a Mechanic Bay Booking App. A bay calendar with jobs as time blocks, job entry with customer, vehicle, service, and hours, a conflict warning for double-booked bays, a today view, and a completed-jobs history with invoice amounts. Built for a garage with two or three bays.
APP07

Farmer Harvest & Sales Ledger

Logs each harvest and each sale, ties sales to crops and buyers, and shows what is still in the field or the store room.

Show prompt
Create an APP (a space that does things, interactive, with its own live database): a Farmer Harvest & Sales Ledger. Harvest entry by crop, field, and quantity, sale entry by buyer and price, a live stock view of what is left, a buyer list with season totals, and income per crop. Simple enough to update in the field.
APP08

Nurse Medication Schedule App

A live medication schedule: who takes what, when, and whether it was given — with a missed-dose alert.

Show prompt
Create an APP (a space that does things, interactive, with its own live database): a Nurse Medication Schedule App. Patient list with medications, doses, and times, a due-now view sorted by next dose, one-tap given marking with timestamp, missed-dose flags, and a daily summary per patient. Clear and calm, built for use during a busy shift.
APP09

Coach Practice Plan Builder

Builds a practice session from a drill library, assigns players to groups, times each block, and saves the plan for reuse.

Show prompt
Create an APP (a space that does things, interactive, with its own live database): a Coach Practice Plan Builder. A drill library with purpose and duration, a session builder that times each block, group assignment for players, saved sessions that can be copied for next week, and a printable session sheet. Built for a coach planning the night before.
APP10

Streamer Clip & Highlight Vault

A vault for clips: save the link, tag the game and the moment, rate it, and pull up the best ones when making a compilation.

Show prompt
Create an APP (a space that does things, interactive, with its own live database): a Streamer Clip & Highlight Vault. Clip entry with title, link, game, and description, tags for funny, clutch, fail, wholesome, and rage, star ratings, filter and search by game or tag, and a best-of list for compilations. Built for a streamer who clips a lot.

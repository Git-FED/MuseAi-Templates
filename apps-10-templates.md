# 10 APP Templates

Apps are spaces that DO things. They are interactive and live — they keep their own data, remember what you typed, and update when you use them. Like a machine with buttons and a memory. Each template below has a ready-to-paste "Ask your Muse" prompt.

---

## 1. Landlord Rent & Repair Tracker

**TYPE:** APP
**For:** A landlord with several units who is tired of chasing rent in group chats and losing repair requests.

**What it does:** Tracks who paid, who is late, and which repairs are open, per unit, per month — with a running balance for each tenant.

**Key features:**
- Tenant list per unit with rent amount, due date, and paid/overdue status
- Repair tickets: tenant reports an issue, landlord marks it in progress or done
- Monthly income total across all units, shown at a glance
- Late-fee calculator that adds the fee after the grace period
- A history view so you can see every payment and repair for any unit

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create an APP (a space that does things, interactive, with its own live
database): a Landlord Rent & Repair Tracker. Tenant list per unit with rent
and paid/overdue status, repair tickets tenants can open and I can close,
monthly income totals, a late-fee calculator, and a full history per unit.
Keep it simple enough to update from my phone.
```

## 2. Food Truck Daily Sales Log

**TYPE:** APP
**For:** A food truck owner who wants to log each day's sales in two minutes at the end of a shift.

**What it does:** A fast daily log: enter what sold, what ran out, and what the till says, and it builds a running picture of the best sellers and the best locations.

**Key features:**
- Quick-entry form: item, quantity sold, and price, with one-tap add
- Location picker so sales are tied to where the truck parked
- Sold-out flag per item per day
- Running totals: best sellers this week, best location this month
- End-of-day cash vs card split with a difference flag if the till is off

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create an APP (a space that does things, interactive, with its own live
database): a Food Truck Daily Sales Log. Quick-entry form for items sold,
a location picker, sold-out flags, running best-seller and best-location
totals, and an end-of-day cash vs card split. Fast enough to finish in
two minutes after a shift.
```

**What it does:** Records donations and pledges, shows progress toward the annual budget, and gives each giver a simple year-to-date total when asked.

**Key features:**
- Donation entry: giver, amount, date, fund (general, building, missions)
- Pledge tracking: promised amount, paid so far, remaining, and pace
- Budget progress bar: how far the year is toward the annual target
- Weekly offering totals with a simple chart
- A giver summary view for end-of-year statements

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create an APP (a space that does things, interactive, with its own live
database): a Church Donation & Pledge Tracker. Donation entry by giver,
amount, date, and fund, pledge tracking with paid vs remaining, a budget
progress bar, weekly offering totals, and a giver summary for year-end
statements. Private and simple for a volunteer treasurer.
```

## 5. Teacher Gradebook & Attendance App

**Key features:**

**Ask your Muse:**

## 6. Mechanic Bay Booking App

**TYPE:** APP
**For:** A small garage with two or three bays that books jobs by phone and keeps double-booking itself.

**What it does:** A booking board: customers pick a day and a bay, jobs are scheduled by length, and the day's work is visible at a glance.

**Key features:**
- Bay calendar: each bay shown as a column, jobs as blocks by time
- Job entry: customer, vehicle, service, estimated hours, and notes
- Conflict warning when a job overlaps another in the same bay
- A "today" view showing what is in each bay right now
- Completed jobs move to a history list with the final invoice amount

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create an APP (a space that does things, interactive, with its own live
database): a Mechanic Bay Booking App. A bay calendar with jobs as time
blocks, job entry with customer, vehicle, service, and hours, a conflict
warning for double-booked bays, a today view, and a completed-jobs history
with invoice amounts. Built for a garage with two or three bays.
```

## 10. Streamer Clip & Highlight Vault

**TYPE:** APP
**For:** A streamer who clips funny or epic moments and loses them in a folder full of files named "clip_034.mp4".

**What it does:** A vault for clips: save the link, tag the game and the moment, rate it, and pull up the best ones when making a compilation.

**Key features:**
- Clip entry: title, link, game, date, and a one-line description
- Tags for moment type: funny, clutch, fail, wholesome, rage
- Star rating per clip
- Filter and search by game, tag, or rating
- A "best of" list that collects the top-rated clips for compilations

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create an APP (a space that does things, interactive, with its own live
database): a Streamer Clip & Highlight Vault. Clip entry with title, link,
game, and description, tags for funny, clutch, fail, wholesome, and rage,
star ratings, filter and search by game or tag, and a best-of list for
compilations. Built for a streamer who clips a lot.
```

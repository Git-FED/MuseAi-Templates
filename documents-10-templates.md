# 10 DOCUMENT Templates

Documents are files: you read them, edit them, print them, send them. They do not run anything. Each template below has a ready-to-paste "Ask your Muse" prompt.

---

## 1. Landlord Move-In Condition Report

**TYPE:** DOCUMENT
**For:** A landlord with 1–20 rental units.

**What it does:** A room-by-room inspection form the landlord and tenant both sign on move-in day, so nobody argues later about who scratched the floor.

**Key features:**
- Room-by-room checklist (walls, floors, windows, appliances, fixtures) with a condition grade per item
- Photo log slots with a place to note the file name of each photo
- Meter readings section for water, electric, and gas on move-in day
- Signature and date lines for landlord and tenant, plus a notes box for anything unusual
- A matching move-out column so the same form is reused at the end of the tenancy

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create a DOCUMENT: a Landlord Move-In Condition Report as a printable document.
Room-by-room checklist with condition grades, photo log slots, utility meter
readings, signature lines for landlord and tenant, and a matching move-out
column. Make it clean and ready to print on letter paper.
```

## 2. Food Truck Weekly Cost Sheet

**TYPE:** DOCUMENT
**For:** A food truck owner who wants to know if the week actually made money.

**What it does:** One page per week that adds up food costs, fuel, permits, and sales, and shows the real profit after every expense.

**Key features:**
- Daily sales table (Monday–Sunday) with cash and card split
- Food and supply cost lines with a column for supplier name
- Fixed costs section: fuel, propane, commissary fee, permit amortisation
- Automatic-style profit summary: gross sales minus all costs, plus profit per hour worked
- A "what to change next week" notes box at the bottom

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create a DOCUMENT: a Food Truck Weekly Cost Sheet as a document with tables.
Daily sales with cash/card split, food and supply costs, fixed costs like fuel
and commissary fees, and a profit summary showing profit per hour worked.
One page per week, ready to print and fill in by hand or on screen.
```

## 3. Church Volunteer Rotation Letter

**TYPE:** DOCUMENT
**For:** A church administrator coordinating greeters, ushers, and nursery workers.

**What it does:** A warm, clear letter sent to each volunteer explaining their rotation, dates, and who to call if they cannot make it.

**Key features:**
- Personal greeting with the volunteer's name and role
- A three-month rotation table with dates, service times, and team partners
- A swap procedure: who to contact and how far in advance
- A short thank-you paragraph that sounds like a person wrote it, not a form
- A tear-off or reply section to confirm the volunteer accepts the schedule

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create a DOCUMENT: a Church Volunteer Rotation Letter as a document.
Personal greeting, a three-month rotation table with dates and team partners,
a swap procedure, a genuine thank-you paragraph, and a reply section to
confirm the schedule. Warm tone, ready to print and mail.
```

## 4. Freelancer Scope-of-Work Agreement

**TYPE:** DOCUMENT
**For:** A freelancer (designer, writer, developer) who needs a short, plain agreement before starting work.

**What it does:** A one-to-two page agreement that says exactly what will be delivered, when, for how much, and what happens if the client changes their mind.

**Key features:**
- Deliverables list written in plain language, not legal jargon
- Payment schedule with deposit, milestones, and final payment
- Two rounds of revisions included, with a price for extra rounds
- A kill-fee clause if the client cancels mid-project
- Signature lines and a start date, with a blank for the client's business name

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create a DOCUMENT: a Freelancer Scope-of-Work Agreement as a document.
Plain-language deliverables list, payment schedule with deposit and milestones,
two included revision rounds, a kill-fee clause, and signature lines. Short
and clear, no legal jargon, ready to send to a client.
```

## 5. Teacher Parent Conference Summary

**TYPE:** DOCUMENT

**What it does:** A half-page form filled in during or right after each conference, so the teacher, the parent, and the school all remember the same plan.

**Key features:**
- Student name, grade, date, and who attended
- Strengths and concerns boxes, each with room for two or three short points
- Agreed actions table: what, who does it, and by when
- A follow-up date already written in before the meeting ends
- Signature lines for teacher and parent

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create a DOCUMENT: a Teacher Parent Conference Summary as a document.
Student details, strengths and concerns boxes, an agreed-actions table with
owner and due date, a follow-up date, and signature lines for teacher and
parent. Half a page, easy to complete in five minutes after a meeting.
```

## 6. Mechanic Repair Estimate & Authorization

**TYPE:** DOCUMENT
**For:** An independent mechanic who wants customers to approve work before the wrench turns.

**Key features:**
- Vehicle details: year, make, model, VIN, mileage
- Parts table with part name, supplier, price, and warranty length
- Labour lines with hours and rate, shown separately from parts
- A clear total, plus a line for the maximum the customer authorizes without a call
- Signature and date for customer authorization, with a "do not proceed" option

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create a DOCUMENT: a Mechanic Repair Estimate & Authorization as a document.
Vehicle details, a parts table with prices and warranties, labour lines with
hours and rate, a total, a maximum-authorized-without-a-call line, and a
customer signature block. Professional and ready to hand to a customer.
```

## 7. Farmer Crop Rotation Plan

**TYPE:** DOCUMENT
**For:** A small farmer planning what grows where over the next three years.

**What it does:** A field-by-field, year-by-year plan that rotates crop families so the soil recovers and pests do not settle in.

**Key features:**
- Field map table: each field listed with its size and soil notes
- Three-year rotation grid by crop family (legume, root, leaf, fruit)
- Cover crop and rest periods marked in the plan
- Notes on what each field grew last year and what it must not grow next
- A harvest window column so planting and selling do not collide

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create a DOCUMENT: a Farmer Crop Rotation Plan as a document.
Field list with size and soil notes, a three-year rotation grid by crop family,
cover crop and rest periods, last-year history per field, and a harvest window
column. Practical and specific to a small farm, not a textbook.
```

## 8. Nurse Shift Handover Note

**TYPE:** DOCUMENT
**For:** A nurse handing over patients at the end of a shift.

**What it does:** A structured handover sheet so the incoming nurse knows every patient's status, risks, and pending tasks without a long verbal ramble.

**Key features:**
- One row per patient: bed, name, diagnosis, and acuity level
- SBAR-style boxes: situation, background, assessment, recommendation
- Pending tasks checklist: meds due, labs ordered, family calls to return
- Watch-list flags for fall risk, allergy, and isolation precautions
- Space for the outgoing nurse's name, time, and signature

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create a DOCUMENT: a Nurse Shift Handover Note as a document.
One row per patient with bed, diagnosis, and acuity, SBAR-style boxes,
a pending tasks checklist, watch-list flags for fall risk, allergy, and
isolation, and a sign-off line. Clear enough to hand over in five minutes.
```

## 9. Coach Season Development Report

- Skill ratings on five coach-chosen skills, with a short comment each
- Attendance and effort notes, kept positive and honest

- A short note to parents about next season's registration dates

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create a DOCUMENT: a Coach Season Development Report as a document.

comments, attendance and effort notes, a three-item off-season practice plan,
and a note to parents about next season registration. Encouraging and honest.
```

## 10. Gamer Tournament Rules Pack

**TYPE:** DOCUMENT
**For:** A gamer organizing a local or online tournament for friends or a community.

**What it does:** A complete rules pack so players know the format, the schedule, what is banned, and how disputes are settled before anyone plays.

**Key features:**
- Tournament format: bracket type, match length, and tiebreaker rules
- Banned items, characters, or settings listed plainly
- Match schedule with round times and a late-arrival rule
- Dispute procedure: who decides, and how evidence is submitted
- Prize split and a code of conduct with consequences for quitting mid-match

**Ask your Muse:**
```
Create a DOCUMENT: a Gamer Tournament Rules Pack as a document.
Bracket format and tiebreakers, banned items or characters, match schedule
with a late-arrival rule, a dispute procedure, prize split, and a code of
conduct. Written for players, not lawyers, ready to share in a group chat.
```

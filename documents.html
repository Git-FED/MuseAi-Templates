Show prompt
Create a DOCUMENT: a Food Truck Weekly Cost Sheet as a document with tables. Daily sales with cash/card split, food and supply costs, fixed costs like fuel and commissary fees, and a profit summary showing profit per hour worked. One page per week, ready to print and fill in by hand or on screen.
